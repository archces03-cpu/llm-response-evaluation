# Project Scope and Limitations

## Purpose

This document explains the scope and limitations of the LLM Response Evaluation Portfolio.

## Dataset Limitations

- The dataset contains only five synthetic examples.
- The examples do not represent the full range of real-world AI tasks.
- The results should not be generalized to all language models or evaluation scenarios.

## Evaluation Limitations

- Ratings are human judgments and may vary between reviewers.
- The scoring rubric is a demonstration tool, not a validated industry benchmark.
- Factual claims should be independently verified when necessary.
- A high average score may conceal a serious error in an individual evaluation dimension.

## Performance Claims

This project demonstrates an evaluation workflow. It does not establish statistical reliability, production-level performance, or measurable improvements in an AI model.

## Data Privacy and Ethics

- No confidential client data or private platform tasks are intentionally included.
- Examples are synthetic and intended for educational and portfolio use.
- Evaluation feedback should be evidence-based, consistent, and respectful of user intent.

## Future Improvements

Future versions could include:

- A larger and more diverse evaluation dataset.
- Additional examples of ambiguous prompts and edge cases.
- Independent reviews by multiple evaluators.
- A documented process for resolving rating disagreements.
- More detailed analysis of recurring error types.

## Conclusion

Recognizing limitations is an important part of responsible AI evaluation. Transparent reporting helps readers understand what a project demonstrates and what conclusions cannot yet be drawn.
