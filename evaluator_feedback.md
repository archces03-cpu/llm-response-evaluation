# Evaluator Feedback: Worked Examples

## Case E004 — Factual Accuracy

**User prompt:** Name one planet closer to the Sun than Earth.

**AI response:** Mars is closer to the Sun than Earth.

**Issue identified:** The response contains a factual error. Mars orbits farther from the Sun than Earth.

**Suggested correction:** Mercury is closer to the Sun than Earth.

**Evaluator feedback:** The response confidently provides an incorrect astronomical fact. Replace Mars with Mercury or Venus and verify the answer before submission.

**Relevant criteria:** Accuracy.

## Case E005 — Instruction-following

**User prompt:** List exactly three benefits of exercise.

**AI response:** Exercise supports cardiovascular health and can improve mood.

**Issue identified:** The response provides two benefits instead of the requested three.

**Suggested correction:** Add a third distinct benefit, such as improved muscular strength.

**Evaluator feedback:** The response is relevant and understandable but does not satisfy the requested number of items. Check explicit quantity requirements before finalizing.

**Relevant criteria:** Instruction-following and relevance.

## Lessons

- Identify the specific problem.
- Explain why it matters.
- Provide a practical correction.
- Tie feedback to the relevant evaluation criterion.
- Avoid vague comments such as "bad answer" or "needs improvement."
